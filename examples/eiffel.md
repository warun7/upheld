The Eiffel Tower was designed by the engineering company of Gustave Eiffel and completed in 1889 [1]. It was originally intended to stand for only 20 years [1]. It was the tallest man-made structure in the world until the Chrysler Building in New York was finished in 1930 [1]. Its design was led by the architect Frank Lloyd Wright [1]. The tower is repainted every three years using a special bronze paint [2].
